## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an academic, editorial, and distinguished visual identity inspired by high-standard secondary education and institutional heritage. It bridges classical scholarly prestige with contemporary digital precision, avoiding the sterile and generic qualities of conventional educational dashboards.

The emotional tone conveys authority, quiet intellect, academic discipline, and open discovery. The design aesthetic balances **Editorial Minimalism** with **Academic Classical Typography**:
- Generous, calculated whitespace evokes the breathing room of curated broadsheet journalism and university publications.
- Clean warm cream foundations eliminate harsh sterile pure whites, replacing them with tactile, archival surfaces.
- A high-contrast typographic pairing of classical, expressive serif headlines alongside ultra-crisp monospaced or modern sans elements.
- Heavy reliance on editorial layout structures (eyebrow category labels, featured spotlight cards, masonry galleries, and sharp hairline rules) rather than soft gradients or heavy floating drop shadows.

## Colors

The color palette centers on tactile, literary warmth complemented by dense intellectual ink:

- **Primary (`#0f172a`)**: The deep inky navy tone used for primary typography, authoritative accents, and key structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#1e293b`)**: Muted slate navy for secondary headers, icons, and subtle brand accents.
- **Tertiary (`#475569`)**: Balanced charcoal-slate utilized for meta descriptions, captions, and secondary metadata.
- **Neutral (`#fbfbf9`)**: Architectural off-white cream that forms the core canvas, eliminating monitor glare and instilling an archival, physical-paper texture.

### Named Color Extensions
- **`surface-cream-subtle` (`#f5f4ef`)**: Secondary background layer for inset containers, featured cards, and table headers.
- **`surface-border` (`#e2e2dc`)**: Low-contrast boundary tone for cards, dividers, and rule lines.
- **`footer-midnight` (`#0a0f1d`) & `footer-navy` (`#0b132b`)**: Inverted grounding block reserved for institutional footers and marquee headers, framing content with monumental contrast.
- **`accent-focus` (`#f5f4ef`)**: High-contrast outline tone engineered specifically for accessibility outlines on dark surfaces.
- **`accent-gold` (`#b45309`)**: Scholastic gold accent reserved for awards, honors, and distinction badges.

## Typography

The typographical cadence establishes an authentic journalistic tone:
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Evokes the prestige of traditional academic publications. Headings use snug letter-spacing and tight line-heights. Italic variants may be introduced selectively for literary emphasis or quote highlights.
- **Body & Functional Labels (`Geist`)**: Chosen for its geometric precision, neutral clarity, and developer-grade legibility at scale. Long-form copy requires generous line heights (between `1.6` and `1.7`) to maximize legibility against the cream background.
- **The Eyebrow Construct**: Key editorial sections must precede headlines with an uppercase, wide-tracked label (`eyebrow-lg` or `eyebrow-sm`). This directly grounds the accompanying serif headline within a crisp categorical structure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a structured **12-column responsive fluid-to-fixed grid** contained within a maximum width of `1280px`, supported by a strict editorial content hierarchy.

### Editorial Rhythm & Layout Models
- **Homepage News Layout**: Bound to a 5-item cap. It utilizes a `1 Spotlight + 4 Grid` arrangement where 1 prominent card spans 7 columns and the remaining 4 entries occupy an adjacent 5-column or sub-grid structure.
- **Archive Cataloging (`/berita`)**: 1 hero spotlight card spanning the full 12 columns, succeeded by a clean 3-column article feed limited to 100 entries per pagination index.
- **Gallery Masonry**: Staggered column configuration (3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile) with precalculated aspect ratio constraints to eliminate dynamic shifts during asset load.
- **Section Dividers**: Sections are articulated by dedicated hairline rules (`1px solid var(--surface-border)`) paired with `space-3xl` (4.5rem) vertical padding, reinforcing an architectural, physical sheet layout.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally eliminates traditional, diffused box shadows in favor of **Tonal Planes and Crisp 1px Hairlines**. This emphasizes editorial precision and avoids generic modern SaaS conventions.

- **Flat Graphic Planes**: Surfaces stay grounded on `#fbfbf9` or `#f5f4ef`. Hierarchy is created through contrast, scale, and border density rather than blur elevation.
- **Crisp Structural Hairlines**: Cards, sidebars, dialogue boundaries, and headers utilize a continuous `1px solid #e2e2dc` or `#e5e7eb` boundary.
- **Midnight Footers & Grounding Blocks**: Full-bleed immersion is achieved by transitioning directly into `#0a0f1d` or `#0b132b` zones. Content within these blocks inverts into high-contrast cream and off-white typography without mid-tone drops or glows.
- **Dialog & Navigation Overlays**: Modals and dropdown flyouts sit atop a high-contrast `#0f172a` backdrop at `40%` opacity, framed by a strict 1px solid border.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft / Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** shape language that preserves clean, editorial corners without appearing razor-sharp or overly harsh:

- **Cards and Containers**: Set to `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius to ensure structural integrity across grid layouts.
- **Social Action Elements & Category Pills**: Explicit exceptions where functional tags and social buttons utilize full pill radii (`9999px`) to create distinction between content surfaces and interactive triggers.
- **Visual Motif Integration**: The school's institutional badge and owl motif (`OwlMotif`) are framed within crisp geometric containers, accentuating heritage through structured framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0f172a` background, `#fbfbf9` typography, 0.25rem radius, generous padding (`0.625rem 1.25rem`). Subtle scale transition on hover (`1.01`) with no shadow expansion.
- **Outline / Pill (Social & Actions)**: 1px border (`#e2e2dc`), transparent background, `#0f172a` label, fully rounded (`rounded-full`). On hover, background shifts to `#f5f4ef`.
- **Focus Rings**: `:focus-visible` triggers a distinct 2px ring offset with high contrast against both cream (`#0f172a` ring) and midnight backgrounds (`#f5f4ef` ring).

### Cards (Editorial News & Facilities)
- **Standard Card**: Crisp `1px solid #e2e2dc` boundary, `#fbfbf9` background, zero elevation. Hover state introduces a clean 1px border color transition to `#0f172a`.
- **Spotlight Card (Kartu Sorotan)**: Dual-pane layout featuring an editorial headline, wide-tracked uppercase category pill, timestamp, and an uncropped 16:10 ratio photograph.
- **Placeholder Fallback Cards**: Clean dotted or dashed hairline (`1px dashed #e2e2dc`) on `#f5f4ef`, housing a muted institutional emblem (`OwlMotif`) during unpopulated data states.

### Eyebrow Labels & Chips
- Set in `Geist`, uppercase with `0.12em` tracking.
- Paired immediately above `Playfair Display` headlines to establish instant categorical grouping.
- Chip variants utilize subtle `#f5f4ef` background fill with a 1px `#e2e2dc` border.

### Input Fields
- Neutral background `#fbfbf9` with a persistent `1px solid #e2e2dc` outline.
- Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state transitions the border to `#0f172a` without drop shadows. Placeholder text set to `#64748b`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square 16px boxes with `0.125rem` radius or circular 16px radios with `1px solid #0f172a`. Checked state fills `#0f172a` with an interior cream checkmark.

### Footer Module
- Inverted anchor block (`#0a0f1d` / `#0b132b`).
- Primary typography shifts to `#fbfbf9`, metadata copy to `#94a3b8`, and divider rules to `#1e293b`.
- Houses secondary navigation, academic affiliations, license notices, and institutional contacts.
